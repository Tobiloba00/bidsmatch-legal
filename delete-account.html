Delete Your Data

BidsMatch.com · Effective October 1, 2026

How to delete your BidsMatch.com account and the data linked to it.

In the app

Open the BidsMatch app, go to More, then Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm. Your profile, projects, bids, messages, reviews, saved items, support requests and uploaded files are removed straight away.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email Support@BidsMatch.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm once it is done.

What we may keep

Payment and invoice records, which we must keep for accounting and tax, and anything needed to prevent fraud or settle a dispute. See our Privacy Policy for the retention periods.

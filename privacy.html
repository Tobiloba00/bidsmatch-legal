Privacy Policy

BidsMatch.com · Effective October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Valeo Investment Group LC doing business as BidsMatch.com ("BidsMatch," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information through the BidsMatch website, application, marketplace, communications, payment tools, AI features, and related services. It should be read with the Terms of Service.

1. Scope and Controller

This Policy applies when BidsMatch determines why and how personal information is processed. Valeo Investment Group LC is the responsible business or controller unless another notice states otherwise. It does not govern a Contractor’s, Client’s, payment processor’s, social-login provider’s, or other third party’s independent practices.

2. Information You Provide

Account and identity information: name, username, email, phone number, age or eligibility confirmation, password credentials or authentication tokens, profile photo, company logo, address, and account type.

Business and professional information: business name, contact person, website, description, service area, work categories, years in business, social links, references, licenses, permits, certifications, insurance, W-9s, and other business documents.

Marketplace information: Jobs, locations, property type, descriptions, budgets, photos, plans, files, deadlines, Bids, Estimates, scopes, measurements, materials, labor, schedules, acceptances, cancellations, reviews, reports, saved items, and transaction status.

Communications and support: messages, attachments, call or support details, surveys, complaints, fraud reports, and other correspondence.

Payment and subscription information: plan, billing cycle, invoice details, transaction amount, status, processor tokens, limited payment method details such as brand and last four digits, refund and chargeback information, tax information, and payout details. Raw card numbers are handled by payment processors, not stored by BidsMatch.

AI and document information: prompts, instructions, uploaded images, measurements, draft messages, estimates, summaries, AI feedback, and documents stored in the AI Doc Vault.

3. Information Collected Automatically

We may collect device and network information such as IP address, device type, operating system, browser, app version, identifiers, language, time zone, crash data, security events, and approximate location derived from IP.

We may collect usage and interaction information such as pages and screens viewed, searches, clicks, filters, referring pages, session timing, feature use, message metadata, notification interactions, and affiliate attribution.

With a just-in-time permission, Elite job features may collect precise GPS coordinates and timestamps for check-in, check-out, on-site verification, fraud prevention, safety, and job-management records. We should not collect continuous background location unless separately disclosed and consented to.

4. Information From Other Sources

We may receive information from social-login providers, identity or credential services, payment processors, calendar or mapping services, analytics and security vendors, marketing partners, affiliates, other Users, public records, and lawful fraud-prevention sources. The information depends on your settings and the third party’s rules.

Other Users may submit information about you in Jobs, Bids, messages, reviews, invoices, reports, or shared documents. If you provide information about another person, you represent that you have authority and have given required notices.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts; manage roles and subscriptions; publish profiles and Jobs; match Clients and Contractors; enable search, bids, messaging, saved items, reviews, calendars, invoices, payments, GPS verification, files, and notifications; provide AI features; administer referrals and commissions; deliver support; and maintain records.

We also use information to authenticate Users; detect fraud, abuse, unsafe conduct, fake reviews, chargebacks, and security incidents; enforce agreements; comply with law; establish or defend claims; improve and test the Platform; measure performance; personalize recommendations; and communicate transactional or legally permitted marketing messages.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on performance of a contract, legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations, protection of vital interests, or consent. Consent may be withdrawn for future processing where applicable, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

6. Public and Marketplace Visibility

Contractor business names, logos, descriptions, categories, service areas, ratings, reviews, and selected professional details may be visible to Users or the public according to Platform settings. Job titles, descriptions, general locations, categories, deadlines, budgets, and submission methods may be visible to eligible Contractors.

Private Client contact details should not be displayed publicly unless the Client chooses or a workflow requires disclosure to an appropriate Contractor. Messages, bids, invoices, precise addresses, payments, and private documents are intended for authorized parties, service providers, and administrators as needed. Users should not put sensitive data into public fields.

7. AI Features

Prompts, images, documents, and other inputs may be processed by BidsMatch and contracted AI or cloud providers to generate requested output, secure the service, evaluate quality, and improve features as described at collection and in vendor agreements. We will not represent that AI inputs are confidential if actual vendor settings or contracts do not support that promise.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, full financial account information, health information, trade secrets, or another person’s sensitive information unless the feature expressly requires it and you are authorized. AI output may reveal or infer information and must be reviewed before sharing. AI may not make final legal or financial commitments without User confirmation.

8. AI Doc Vault and Sensitive Business Documents

AI Doc Vault files may include insurance, licenses, certifications, W-9s, contracts, and business records. They are private to the owner unless deliberately shared, required for a transaction, disclosed to a service provider operating the feature, or disclosed for security, legal compliance, or protection of rights.

We should apply access controls, encryption where appropriate, logging, and expiration reminders. Users must still keep original records and control who may receive them. The Vault is not a legal records custodian, tax advisor, insurance verifier, or guaranteed backup service.

9. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to other Users when necessary for profiles, Jobs, Bids, messages, selected-contractor workflows, invoices, payments, calendars, reviews, reports, or other requested interactions.

We may disclose information to vendors and processors that provide hosting, storage, authentication, identity verification, communications, customer support, analytics, fraud prevention, AI, maps, calendars, file processing, payment processing, billing, tax, and professional services. They should receive only information reasonably necessary and be bound by appropriate terms.

We may disclose information to payment networks, banks, Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, insurers, auditors, advisors, regulators, courts, law enforcement, or other parties when reasonably necessary to process a transaction, comply with law, protect safety, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, or establish and defend legal claims.

Information may be transferred in a merger, financing, acquisition, bankruptcy, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law. We may disclose aggregated or deidentified information that is not reasonably linkable to a person and will not attempt to reidentify it except as permitted for validation.

9A. Essential Plan Data Practices

When an Essential User receives immediate or priority marketplace access, we process account role, plan entitlement, subscription status, Job posting time, access eligibility, categories, service area, search criteria, filters, saved items, ranking or matching signals, and interaction data to determine when and how eligible opportunities appear. Search and marketplace activity may also be used for fraud prevention, service analytics, troubleshooting, and product improvement.

Invoicing may process Contractor and Client identifiers, business and contact information, logo, address, website, Job reference, invoice number, issue and due dates, descriptions, quantities, rates, taxes, discounts, totals, notes, status, attachments, viewing activity, delivery events, and PDF files. Relevant invoice information is shared with the parties to the invoice, payment providers, and vendors necessary to create, deliver, store, secure, and support it.

Payment features may process payer and payee identifiers, amount, currency, invoice or Job reference, payment-provider tokens and identifiers, limited payment-method details, billing address, status, timestamps, refunds, disputes, chargebacks, fraud signals, and payout information. Payment processors independently collect payment credentials under their own privacy notices. BidsMatch does not store raw card numbers.

Calendar booking may process event title, description, attendees, contact details, Job or Bid reference, date, time, time zone, location, reminders, status, rescheduling and cancellation history, and if connected calendar provider identifiers and tokens. Event information is shared with invited Users and a connected calendar provider as necessary. Users control whether to connect an external calendar and should avoid placing unnecessary sensitive information in event titles or descriptions.

Customer support interactions may process account and subscription information, communications, attachments, technical logs, device details, screen or error information, and actions taken to investigate or resolve a request. Support records may be retained for quality, training, fraud prevention, dispute, and legal purposes consistent with the retention schedule.

9B. Elite Plan Data Practices

FSM may process Job and customer identifiers, assignments, worker or team member identifiers, schedules, service addresses, status changes, arrival and departure times, notes, photos, documents, estimates, invoices, payment status, audit history, and related communications. Information is shared with authorized participants in the relevant Job and service providers that operate the module. Account administrators are responsible for configuring access and ensuring they have authority to enter information about workers, Clients, properties, and other people.

GPS Job Tracking may process precise coordinates, accuracy radius, timestamps, device and operating-system information, permission status, Job identifier, Contractor or worker identifier, check-in and check out events, and fraud or integrity signals. When the feature is used, job-related location may be shown to the relevant Client, Contractor account, authorized administrators, and vendors needed to provide mapping, storage, security, and verification. We do not use Elite GPS data for unrelated advertising or sell it. We should collect location only after a just-in-time permission and retain it only for the stated operational, dispute, fraud prevention, security, and legal periods.

AI Doc Vault may process document names, categories, files, extracted text and metadata, owner and sharing permissions, issue and expiration dates, notes, reminders, access logs, and AI-generated labels or summaries. Vault records may include sensitive business and tax information. We use them to store, organize, search, remind, secure, and support the feature. They are private to the owner unless deliberately shared, processed by contracted providers, or disclosed for security or legal reasons.

AI Assistant may process prompts, conversation history, Job, Bid, Estimate, invoice, calendar, document, profile, and message content that the User chooses or authorizes the feature to access, together with generated output, feedback, safety signals, and usage metadata. Contracted AI and cloud providers may process this information to generate output and operate, secure, monitor, and improve the service as permitted by their contracts and our notices. BidsMatch should not use private Elite content for unrelated model training unless that practice is separately disclosed and supported by any required consent.

Advanced analytics may process and derive metrics from Job views, searches, profile activity, Bid activity, response time, awards, schedules, invoices, payments, service status, feature use, and other business records. Analytics can include aggregated, inferred, or predicted information. We use it to provide dashboards, evaluate service performance, prevent fraud, improve features, and support Users. Analytics are not sold as credit reports and should not be used by BidsMatch to make legally significant decisions without appropriate notice, review, and safeguards.

Priority support may process the same support information described for Essential, plus plan entitlement, queue priority, feature records needed to investigate Elite modules, and the history of escalations or resolutions. Priority status does not change the privacy choices or security standards applicable to the underlying data.

Data may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for tax, accounting, contract, warranty, payment, fraud, security, litigation-hold, or other legal obligations.
